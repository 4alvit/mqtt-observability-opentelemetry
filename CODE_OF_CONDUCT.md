# Code of Conduct

We welcome participation regardless of background or experience. Be respectful, focus criticism on the work, consider accessibility, and give others room to contribute. Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexualized conduct, unwanted repeated contact and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

This applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate material, warn a participant, limit interaction, or exclude someone temporarily or permanently according to the severity and repeated nature of the behavior. Decisions should protect participants and be proportionate.

## Confidential reports

Report conduct concerns privately to **alvit.work@gmail.com**. Include relevant links, what happened and how to contact you safely; share only information necessary to investigate. The primary maintainer reviews reports, aims to acknowledge them within 14 days, and keeps identifying information confidential except where disclosure is necessary to address the incident or required by law. Retaliation against a good-faith report is prohibited.

The same address can receive a request to reconsider a decision with additional evidence. This project currently has a single maintainer and no independent moderator or appeal panel; do not send a complaint about that maintainer assuming an independent recipient. GitHub's own abuse-reporting process remains available for conduct on its platform. Technical vulnerabilities should use the separate private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
